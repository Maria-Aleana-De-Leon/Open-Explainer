## What the MIT licence covers

Open Explainer's MIT licence ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) covers its code, documentation and prompts, and the demo assets
generated by this repository's own scripts: the "Pip" demo mascot (`tools/make_demo_mascot.py`), the demo sounds and
music (`tools/make_demo_audio.py`) and the documentation images.

It does not cover the Poppins font files above (SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in
`src/openexplainer/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`) or the third-party dependencies below, which are installed separately
under their own licences.
