# Step 01 — Create the master mascot

Use my uploaded photo as the visual reference to create one full-body 2D cartoon mascot of me for educational explainer videos.

Character requirements:

- Preserve my recognizable facial features, skin tone, hairstyle and hair color.
- Use clean, modern vector-style illustration with consistent outlines and simple shading.
- Give the character a natural, friendly expression.
- Full-body, standing straight, facing the camera.
- Arms relaxed at the sides.
- Wear a simple outfit based on my reference photo.
- Both hands, both feet and the entire hairstyle must be visible.
- Transparent background.
- Center the character with comfortable space around the body.

This character will become the MASTER CHARACTER REFERENCE for all future animations.

Use a neutral, front-facing pose suitable for lip synchronization.

Generate only one character, not a character sheet or multiple variations.

Do not include text, accessories that are not in the reference, extra people, or decorative backgrounds.
