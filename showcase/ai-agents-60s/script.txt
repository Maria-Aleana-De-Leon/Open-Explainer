Imagine telling your computer: plan my trip to Tokyo. And instead of just answering, it actually does it.

That is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent. A chatbot talks. An agent takes action, step by step, until the job is done.

Every agent runs the same simple loop. It looks at the goal, makes a plan, takes an action, and then checks the result.

The actions come from tools. A web search, a calendar, a code runner, even your email. The agent picks the right tool for each step.

So for your trip, it searches for flights, compares the prices, books the best one, and adds it to your calendar.

And if something goes wrong, like a sold out flight, it notices, rethinks, and tries again. That loop is what makes it an agent.

Good agents also know when to stop and ask you, especially before they spend your money or send a message.

So an AI agent is a model, plus tools, plus a loop. Follow for more simple AI explainers.
